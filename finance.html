Business problem

Senior stakeholders need one clear view of financial performance without relying on disconnected spreadsheets or static monthly reports.

Analysis focus

Revenue trend, gross profit, margin %, budget vs actual, year-on-year comparison, product/customer contribution and exception analysis.

What I will show

Data preparation, model design, KPI logic, dashboard design choices, key findings and recommended business actions.
