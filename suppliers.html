Case Study

From business question to insight.

The page is designed so we can add the real dataset, DAX, SQL and insights later without rebuilding the website.

Business problem

Purchasing teams need a consistent way to compare suppliers, identify delivery and quality risks and prioritise supplier-management actions.

Analysis focus

On-time delivery, lead time, purchase value, rejected quantity, supplier quality, open commitments and overall supplier ranking.

What I will show

Data preparation, model design, KPI logic, dashboard design choices, key findings and recommended business actions.
